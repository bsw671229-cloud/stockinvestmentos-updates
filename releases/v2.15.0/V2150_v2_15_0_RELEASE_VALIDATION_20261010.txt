Implementation:
Single-market failed-item retry requires an explicit button click and a complete
combined-run snapshot in the same client/account/mode/query/version. Every record
must still be the captured object and carry valid status/timing metadata.
Snapshot and each item are limited to five minutes; a retry never refreshes the
age of retained successful items. Changed or malformed context clears the combined
report and requires a fresh full check. UI disabling is backed by invocation checks.
Failed buying power reuses only a valid quote at most 60 seconds old; otherwise
quote is refreshed first. Fresh quote forces new buying power. Quote failure skips
dependent buying power while a failed independent balance still runs.
Calls remain serial through the unchanged broker adapter and its existing limits.
No automatic polling, shared cache, new persistence or additional render request.
Summary records expose original timestamps, elapsed time, new/retained provenance,
and fixed Korean next-action guidance. Dual-market summary gains guidance only;
the failed-item button and phase progress belong to the selected-market view.
Downloaded reports extend the existing allowlist with item enums only. Raw results,
exceptions, account identity, credentials, positions, prices and amounts stay out.
Progress callback failure does not interrupt independent inquiries.
